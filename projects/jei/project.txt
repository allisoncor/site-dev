When Radeus Labs bought Jerauld Electronics Incorporated (Jei) in 2024, the new ownership wanted a new logo for their website and press releases. I was given Jei's original logo from 2014, which had become so pixelated and low quality that it was hard to use, and was asked to make it usable again.

Through the transition, Jei stopped carrying its full product line and focused only on its recording devices. Radeus wanted to keep a lot of what Jei stood for, including its bright red, so I rebuilt the logo as a clean, sharp mark for that more focused direction while keeping the color and character the brand was known for.
