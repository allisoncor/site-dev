A 25-page end of year report I designed as a Graphic Designer for Associated Students, Inc. at CSUSB, summing up the 2025-2026 year across every department: the Board of Directors, Finance, Elections, the Corporate Office, Howl Squad, Legislative Affairs, Graphics and Marketing, Programs, internships and student success stories.

ASI asked for a blue and white design that fit the campus and the ASI brand. Working from the content each department sent and the year's event photos, I designed an information-heavy report that reads clearly and stays consistent from cover to close.
